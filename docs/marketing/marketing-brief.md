# Casewise — Marketing Brief

For: anyone (human or AI) writing marketing content — LinkedIn posts, landing copy,
outreach messages. Self-contained; no other project file is needed.

## What it is (one line)

Casewise (https://casewise.si) is an AI research assistant for licensed attorneys
practicing New York property law — every answer is cited to, quoted from, and
verified against the actual New York statutes, court decisions, and DHCR guidance.

## Who it's for

Solo and small-firm attorneys practicing NY property / landlord-tenant /
housing-court law. NOT consumers, NOT tenants/landlords themselves, NOT BigLaw.
The buyer does their own research today using Google, generic AI chatbots they
don't trust, or expensive Westlaw/Lexis subscriptions they underuse.

## The problem we solve

1. Generic AI chatbots invent citations — attorneys have been sanctioned for
   filing AI-fabricated cases (Mata v. Avianca, $5,000; $145k+ in sanctions
   across federal circuits in Q1 2026 alone). Attorneys want AI speed but can't
   risk fake law.
2. Research takes hours an attorney can't bill fairly or doesn't have.
3. Westlaw/Lexis cost hundreds per month and are built for BigLaw workflows.

## Why Casewise is different (the claims we CAN make)

- **Closed library, not the internet.** Answers come only from a curated corpus of
  NY primary sources: the Consolidated Laws, ten years of court decisions, DHCR
  guidance, the Uniform Civil Rules, and official forms. The tool never browses.
- **Fabricated citations are structurally impossible.** The AI can only cite
  documents actually retrieved from the library; anything else is stripped before
  the attorney sees it. (Say "structurally impossible for a cited source to not
  exist" — do NOT say "never wrong" or "hallucination-free".)
- **Every quotation is checked word-for-word** against the stored source text.
  Exact matches are marked verified; anything else is flagged "not an exact
  match — check wording" instead of silently shown.
- **Building-grounded answers (NYC):** mention an address and the answer is
  grounded in that building's real record — rent-stabilization indicators, HPD
  violations, owner portfolio, eviction filings.
- **IRAC drafting:** give it the issue + your case facts and it produces a
  structured Issue/Rule/Application/Conclusion draft with verified citations,
  counteranalysis, and explicit [FACT NEEDED] flags where facts are missing —
  exportable to Word with a verification appendix. No competitor brands this.
- **Confidential by architecture:** questions are never stored on our servers —
  research history lives only in the attorney's own browser. Nothing is used to
  train any model. Documents checked are processed in memory, never saved.
- **Point-in-time research:** answer as of a past date (decisions after it
  excluded, amended statutes flagged).
- **Check cites:** paste or upload any brief/motion/memo (yours or opposing
  counsel's) and every NY citation is validated against official records;
  quotes are matched to sources.

## Hard rules (compliance — never violate in any post)

- Casewise is a research tool FOR licensed attorneys. It is NOT legal advice and
  is never marketed to the public for self-help.
- Never claim it replaces attorney judgment or review; outputs are drafts/research
  the attorney verifies (the product makes verification fast — that's the pitch).
- Never say "hallucination-free", "always accurate", or "unlimited".
- Don't disparage Westlaw/Lexis by name beyond price/fit comparisons.
- Confidentiality claims must match reality exactly as stated above (no storage
  of questions; browser-only history; no training on user data).
